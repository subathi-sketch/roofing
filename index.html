Questions

Common Roofing Questions

What affects the price of a new roof?

Roof size and pitch, the material you choose, how many layers need removing, any rotted decking and ease of access all play a part. We inspect in person so the estimate reflects your real roof.

Can you repair a leak quickly?

Yes. For active leaks we provide fast temporary protection, followed by a permanent repair once conditions are safe and dry.

Do you work with insurance companies?

We document storm damage with photos and a written report and are happy to meet your adjuster at the property. Inspections are always free.

What if you find hidden damage during the job?

If we discover soft or rotted decking, we stop, show you photos and agree on any extra cost with you before continuing.
