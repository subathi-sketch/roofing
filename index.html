FAQs

Roofing FAQ

How much does a new roof cost?

Every roof is different. Size, pitch, number of layers, material, and access all affect price. A typical asphalt shingle replacement on an average single-family home often falls in the $8,000 to $16,000 range. The only way to get an exact number is a free inspection, after which we give you a written quote you can plan around.

Do you offer emergency roof repair?

Yes. We offer same-day emergency tarping and temporary repairs to protect your home from water damage after a storm or sudden leak.

Will you help with my insurance claim?

Yes. We document damage with dated photos, provide a detailed report, and can meet your adjuster on the roof. We never charge for the inspection.

Will my price change once the work starts?

Our goal is a quote that holds. If we find rotted decking or other hidden damage once the old roof is off, we show you photos and discuss it with you before any change is made.

What warranty do you provide?

You receive the manufacturer's material warranty plus our written workmanship warranty on every installation.
