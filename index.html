Questions

Common Roofing Questions

What affects the price of a new roof?

The main factors are roof size and pitch, the material you choose, how many layers need removing, any rotted decking, and how easy the roof is to access. We inspect in person so the estimate reflects your actual roof, not a guess.

Can you repair a leak quickly?

Yes. For active leaks we offer fast temporary protection, followed by a permanent repair once conditions are safe and dry.

Do you work with insurance companies?

We document storm damage with photos and a written report, and we are happy to meet your adjuster at the property. Inspections are always free.

What if you find hidden damage during the job?

If we discover soft or rotted decking once the old roofing is removed, we stop, show you photos and agree on the extra cost with you before continuing.

What does your warranty cover?

Manufacturers cover the roofing materials. We add our own written workmanship warranty that covers installation errors.
